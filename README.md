# Siftly

**Keep the memories. Sift the clutter.**

Siftly is an Android app that clears event posters, forwards (greetings, chain
messages, ads) and memes out of your WhatsApp images, while leaving photos of
family and friends where they are.

- Reads English, Urdu and Arabic text and recognises how images look, all on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.
- Tabs for Posters, Forwards, Memes, Not sure and Kept. Tap to select; hold to view and label.
- Learns from your choices: what you clear, re-label and unselect.
- Clutter is moved to `Pictures/Siftly/Posters`, `Forwards` and `Memes` (with undo), or deleted.

## Install

1. On your phone, open the **Releases** page of this repository and download `Siftly.apk`.
2. Open it and allow installs from your browser.
3. Open Siftly and give it "Allow access to manage all files".

Every push to `main` builds a new version automatically. New versions install over the old one
(including over Poster Sorter). Needs Android 11 or later.
